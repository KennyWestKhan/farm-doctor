The solution

Implementation & scalability

Reaches the underserved — and compounds

The moat: a data flywheel

→Farmer runs a diagnosis
→Applies the treatment
→Reports "did it work?"
→Region-by-region evidence improves the next recommendation
The tool gets smarter the more Ghana uses it — a local evidence base that doesn't exist anywhere else.
No app store.

A PWA — opens from a link or QR, works offline on cheap phones. Reaches farmers who'll never download a 50 MB app on a metered connection.

B2B2C model.

Free for farmers. Agro-input companies, government extension, NGOs and cooperatives pay for verified reach and aggregate efficacy insight.

Roadmap.

More crops & regions, disease reference photos, voice input for non-readers.
